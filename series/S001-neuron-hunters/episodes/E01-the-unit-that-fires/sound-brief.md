# E01 sound brief — G3 aligned

Derived from C units that mention observation, signal, record, construction — plus G3 accident-test.

## Law

Sound may present. Sound may not conclude.
Mute the dialogue: the bed must not say lived / died / neuron / machine-succeeded.

## Objects

- **Room:** dry. Constant low hiss. No cinematic score that arrives before evidence.
- **SIGNAL:** short burst, repeatable, unsweetened. Keep organism, noise, memory, and wish all possible. Never heartbeat-design it.
- **Index tone:** once, small, near the STEP 01 stamp. Not a UI lesson.
- **Apparatus:** mechanical start, mark, stop. Printer / gate / recorder unnamed (U-02).
- **Return tone:** same palette as neither accept nor error. Uncaptioned.
- **Trace (optional, sc.5):** quieter than SIGNAL, not a second firing. Must not read as ghost-proof.
- **Silence:** after any line that sounds like proof, longer than the line.
- **Final absence:** SIGNAL does not return. Room continues. No button.

## Do not

- Sweet-tune SIGNAL into “alive.”
- Caption the apparatus.
- Score FIELD’s “neuron” line.
- Requiem the Life/Death question.
- End on a sequel sting.
