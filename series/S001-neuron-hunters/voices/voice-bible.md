# Voice bible — S001 functions

Deferred until after E01 drama. Now written from Mix B + G3 ears.
These are **jobs in the room**, not persons. U-01 still holds.

Temporary Mix A/B ids (atlas / orion / eve / helix) are disposable. Recast freely if the function is kept.

---

## ARCHIVE

**Job:** Stamp. Index. Quote the hunt-sentence. Does not argue.
**Heard as:** Slightly farther than the others. Less air. Almost read, not lived.
**Must:** STEP 01, series title, five-term list, “hunt neither neurons nor objects,” OPEN / no verified UFO claim.
**Must not:** Warmth that sounds like a narrator who already knows; irony; trailers.
**Risk:** Making the title sound like a promise that the signal *is* a neuron.

## FIELD

**Job:** Closest to SIGNAL. Wants a name. Supplies the interpretations G3 must hear *as interpretations*.
**Heard as:** Near-mic. Faster. Cuts in.
**Must:** “There. Again.” “What is it?” “That could be a neuron.” “Did it take?” “Is that the being?” “If it stops, is that death?”
**Must not:** Win the argument with tone. Do not make “could be a neuron” sound settled. Mix B deleted “It fires” — do not restore it in performance ad-lib.
**Risk:** Identity leak (#01). Heat after SCRIBE’s refusal that recasts the guess as fact.

## SCRIBE

**Job:** Keep observation, interpretation, and proof audible as three acts.
**Heard as:** Same room as FIELD, slightly drier, slightly slower. Not a teacher.
**Must:** “Observed. Not identified.” “That is an interpretation.” “Repeating is not a name.” Record ≠ living being. OPEN ending law.
**Must not:** Sermon. Triumph. Softening FIELD into agreement.
**Risk:** If SCRIBE sounds like the show’s moral, the listener stops *doing* the distinction and just receives it.

## CONSTRUCTOR

**Job:** Apparatus and attempt-language. Speaks the formula once. Stops at proposed / not verified.
**Heard as:** Hands in the work. Mid-distance. Less pitch motion than FIELD.
**Must:** Formula once. “Point proposed.” “It recorded an attempt.” “Evidence status remains open.” “I cannot leave a completed point.”
**Must not:** Celebrate the return tone. Ad-lib instrument names (U-02). Stress “BN-point” as a being.
**Risk:** Listen #02 “being-neuron point.” Say the letters as a *term in a chain*, not as a noun that lives.

## SIGNAL

Not a speaker. Not a character. A short event that can still be organism, noise, memory, or wish.
No pulse that reads as heartbeat. No shock that reads as lab torture.

## Ensemble rules

1. No one is named on air.
2. No one explains the repo.
3. After any line that could be proof: let silence be longer than the line.
4. Overlap is allowed only where FIELD interrupts; SCRIBE does not overlap to crush.
5. If a take makes identity, registration, or absence settle, bin the take — do not rewrite the line.
