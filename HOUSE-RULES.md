# House rules

1. Observation ≠ Interpretation ≠ Proof. The three remain audible as separate acts.
2. Canon precedes invention. Existing Funebra construction is source. Episodes extend it; they do not overwrite it for convenience.
3. Copy provenance, not rewrite history. Quote the source unit. Record repo / path / SHA.
4. Sound is argument. Pronunciation, silence, multilingual slip, mechanical noise, and failed recording are narrative.
5. No borrowed power-fantasy spine (rank, chosen-one reveal, humiliation ladder) unless studied as an object.
6. Each episode leaves residue: a question, a constructed object, a failed test, or a new observation.
7. Series numbers are permanent. Catalogue IDs do not recycle.
8. Voice bibles follow drama. They do not define voices before E01 has been heard in draft.
9. `verifiedUfoClaim` in source is `false` on every LS110600 record. E01 may not close that claim.
10. `evidenceStatus` in source is `OPEN`. E01 ends open.
